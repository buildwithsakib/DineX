# DineX User Guide

## For Restaurant Owners

1. **Login** at `/admin/restaurant/login.php`
2. **Complete profile** – update restaurant details.
3. **Add tables** – Tables section.
4. **Generate QR** – QR Codes section, select table, generate, print QR.
5. **Manage menu** – Categories, Cuisines, Foods.
6. **Manage staff** – Staff section (Owner only). Create Manager/Cashier accounts.
7. **View orders** – Orders section, update status.
8. **Kitchen display** – Kitchen section for chefs.
9. **Games & Coupons** – Activate games, configure rewards, generate coupons.
10. **Analytics** – Dashboard and Analytics for insights.

## For Customers

1. Scan QR code on table.
2. Browse menu, search, filter.
3. Add items to cart.
4. Place order.
5. Play games (if available) to win coupons/discounts.
6. View bill and pay (simulated).

## For Founder

1. Login at `/admin/founder/login.php`
2. Approve/activate restaurants.
3. Manage subscription plans and features.
4. Assign subscriptions to restaurants.
5. Override feature access per restaurant.
6. Monitor platform analytics and audit logs.
